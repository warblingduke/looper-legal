Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • Looper works without an account — no sign-up, no email, no password.
  • Your rounds, stats, and profile are stored on your device and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud.
  • We run no server and hold no database of users. We cannot see your data.
  • We never sell or share your personal information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no third-party analytics.
  • A few features send limited data to Apple and to course-data services so the app can function (weather, maps, course downloads, elevation). Details below.
  • If you confirm a fix to a course map, that anonymous course fix (never your rounds, scores, or identity) can be shared with other Looper golfers. You can turn this off in Settings.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Looper app (“Looper,” the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles, why, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Looper iPhone and Apple Watch apps.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Looper is an independent golf app. The developer of Looper is the party responsible for the App (the “data controller” under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR). Because Looper stores your information on your own device and in your own iCloud, you retain direct control of it at all times. For any privacy question or request, contact us at loopergolfbeta@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Looper is designed to collect as little as possible, and to keep what it does collect on your device.

Information you provide

  • Your golf profile — your handicap or scoring average, the clubs in your bag, your calibrated distances, and app preferences you set during onboarding.
  • Your rounds — the holes, shots, clubs, scores, putts, and any notes you record, plus the analytics Looper computes from them.

Information collected automatically while you use the App

  • Precise location (GPS). During a round, Looper uses your location to measure distances, draw the hole as you walk, and log where each shot ended up. Location is used only during an active round and only with your permission. If you enable in-round background tracking, GPS continues while your phone is pocketed during a round and stops when the round ends — there is no location collection outside of active play.
  • Motion & activity. Looper may use your device’s motion sensors to detect whether you are walking or riding in a cart, so it can keep GPS accurate when it matters and conserve battery when it doesn’t. This is processed on your device.
  • Apple Watch & health data (Watch app only). If you play with the Apple Watch app, Looper runs a workout session so the round stays active on your wrist and can optionally recognize swings. Related health/motion data is read on your device to support the round; it is not transmitted to us.

Looper does not collect your contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any device identifiers used for tracking.

What we don’t collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, or account credentials (there is no account). We do not use analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not build a profile of you for advertising.

3. How we use information

We use the information above solely to provide and improve the App’s features — measuring distances, generating shot recommendations, drawing and confirming your holes, computing your stats and trends, and keeping your data available across your own devices via iCloud if you enable it. We do not use your information for advertising or for any purpose unrelated to running the App.

5. How your information is stored and shared

On your device. Your golf data is stored locally and protected by iOS Data Protection encryption.

In your private iCloud (optional). Looper can back up your rounds and profile to your own private iCloud database using Apple’s CloudKit, so your data survives reinstalling the App or switching phones. This is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. Your synced data is tied to your Apple ID and accessible only to you; Looper operates no server and cannot access it. Apple’s handling of iCloud is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Service providers. To function, Looper connects to a small number of services. None of them receives your golf log or any identity for you:

Requests to course-data and elevation services describe the course, not you — they carry no account, name, or golf history.

Community course fixes (optional)

When you confirm or correct a course’s hole layout (for example, confirming the hole routing or the number of holes at a course that isn’t fully mapped), Looper can share that course fix with other golfers through a shared public database (Apple CloudKit) so everyone gets a better map. What is shared is only the map correction itself — the course, the correction, and an anonymous, one-way-hashed device identifier used to prevent duplicates. It never includes your rounds, scores, shots, location history, or any identity. You can turn this off at any time in Settings (“Share your course fixes”); the App works fully without it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose your information to data brokers. We may disclose information only if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device (and, if enabled, in your own iCloud), you control how long it is kept. You can delete an individual round in the App, or use Settings to export or delete all of your data at any time. Deleting the App removes its local data from your device; deleting your data within the App also removes it from your iCloud if sync was enabled. We do not retain copies, because we hold none.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights. Because your data is stored on your own device and in your own iCloud, you can exercise most of them directly in the App at any time. For anything you can’t do in-app, contact loopergolfbeta@gmail.com and we will help.

EU/UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent (for example, by turning off the location permission). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Looper does not sell or share your personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we honor recognized opt-out signals (such as Global Privacy Control) accordingly. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

8. Security

Looper relies on iOS Data Protection to encrypt your data at rest on your device, and on Apple’s encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for the service calls described above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we maintain no central database of users, there is no server-side store of Looper users’ information that could be breached.

9. International users

Looper stores your data on your device and in your iCloud. Where features rely on Apple services (weather, maps, iCloud), Apple may process that data on servers in various countries in accordance with Apple’s own privacy terms. We do not otherwise transfer your personal information internationally, because we do not collect it centrally.

10. Children’s privacy

Looper is a general-audience golf app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact loopergolfbeta@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date, and we will keep the App’s App Store privacy details in sync. Material changes will be reflected before they take effect.
