Support

Questions, bugs, or a course map that needs fixing — we read everything.

Email loopergolfbeta@gmail.com and include your iPhone/Watch model and, for GPS questions, the course and hole number. We aim to reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

A yardage looked wrong. Looper's distances come from GPS plus open course maps. Tree cover and weather can cost a few yards of GPS accuracy; if a mapped feature (green, bunker, tee) is in the wrong place, tell us the course and hole — course maps improve with every report and with the in-app course fixes you can confirm yourself.

A course is missing or partly mapped. Search covers thousands of courses, but some have distance-only coverage. You can confirm hole routing in the app to upgrade a course for everyone, or email us the course name and we'll prioritize it.

Where is my data? On your device, and (if enabled) in your own private iCloud. There's no account and no server of ours holding your rounds. Export or delete everything anytime in Settings. Details: Privacy Policy.

Battery. A full 18 with continuous GPS uses meaningful battery. Start with a good charge; the app stops all location use the moment the round ends.
